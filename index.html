About The Workshop

One workshop. Every piece of iron your farm needs.

JBM Engineering specializes in agricultural machinery manufacturing, machine repair, and custom iron fabrication. We build durable farm equipment, restore damaged machines, and fabricate high-quality iron products such as gates, sheds, trailers, and structural works. Every project is completed with precision, strength, and a commitment to lasting quality.
